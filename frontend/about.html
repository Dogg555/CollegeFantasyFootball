About

Built for college fantasy managers

College Fantasy Football gives private leagues the tools they need for drafts, rosters, waivers, trades, weekly matchups, and standings.

What It Is

The app is a closed-beta fantasy football platform focused on the college game. Managers can create leagues, search the player pool, draft rosters, manage weekly decisions, and track league history.

Beta Focus

The current priority is making the full tester journey reliable: signup, league creation, league joining, draft, lineup management, matchups, scoring, standings, and commissioner workflows.
